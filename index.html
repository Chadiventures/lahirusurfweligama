meet your instructor

Lahiru Prasad

Lahiru Prasad is a local surf instructor born in Ahangama, Sri Lanka. He has spent more than half his life in the water along the south coast. For the past ten years he has taught surfing in Weligama, one of the most forgiving waves in Asia, welcoming students from every corner of the world.

His lessons are built for two kinds of surfers: those catching their first wave, and those ready to push into faster sections and sharper positioning. Beyond the bay, Lahiru guides intermediate surfers along the reef and beach breaks from Madiha and Mirissa through Weligama, Midigama, Ahangama and Kabalana, the same stretch of coast he grew up surfing.

the bay

Sri Lanka's easiest wave to learn on

A long, slow beach break wrapped by two headlands, and the reason most people start here.

Weligama means "sandy village," and its wide, curved bay is exactly that: a long stretch of sand wrapped by two headlands that soften almost every swell before it reaches shore. The waves peel slowly and predictably left and right off a sandbar, giving beginners time to find their feet and intermediate surfers a forgiving wall to practice turns on.

The main season runs roughly from November to April, when south-west monsoon swells wrap into the bay. The water sits warm year round, usually in the high twenties Celsius, so a rash guard is normally all you need. On smaller or off-season days, Weligama still holds a rideable wave more often than most of the south coast, which is why it has become Sri Lanka's most popular place to learn.

Questions about surfing in Weligama, Sri Lanka

Where is the best place to learn to surf in Sri Lanka?

Weligama Bay on Sri Lanka's south coast is one of the best places in the country to learn to surf. The wave is a long, slow beach break sheltered by two headlands, which makes it forgiving for first-timers.

How much do surf lessons cost in Weligama, Sri Lanka?

A private beginner lesson with Lahiru Prasad is 25 USD for 75 minutes, and a private intermediate lesson is 35 USD for 90 minutes in the water. Group lessons for 2 to 4 people cost 20 to 30 USD per person. Every lesson includes a rash guard, a surfboard and a coconut.

Is Weligama good for beginners learning to surf?

Yes. Weligama is one of the most beginner-friendly surf spots in Sri Lanka. The bay is sheltered by two headlands, which softens the swell into a long, slow, predictable beach break.

When is the best time to surf in Sri Lanka?

The main surf season on Sri Lanka's south coast is November to April. Weligama's sheltered bay often holds a rideable wave outside those months too, which is why it is a reliable place to take lessons year-round.

Where is Lahiru Prasad based?

Lahiru Prasad teaches surf lessons in Weligama, on Sri Lanka's south coast, and guides surf tours from Madiha and Mirissa through Weligama, Midigama, Ahangama and Kabalana.

Do the guided surf tours suit beginners?

No. The guided tours are for intermediate surfers who can already catch and ride a green wave. Lahiru takes small groups or solo surfers by tuk-tuk to reef and beach breaks around Madiha, Mirissa, Weligama, Midigama, Ahangama and Kabalana.

How do I get to Weligama from Colombo?

Weligama is on Sri Lanka's south coast, about 2.5 hours south of Colombo by car or train.

What should I bring to a surf lesson in Weligama?

Bring yourself and swimwear. Lahiru provides the surfboard and rash guard for every lesson, and a fresh coconut afterwards.
