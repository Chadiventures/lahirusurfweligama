Meet Lahiru Prasad

Lahiru Prasad was born in Ahangama and has spent more than half his life in the water along Sri Lanka's south coast. For the past ten years he has taught surfing in Weligama, one of the most forgiving and rewarding waves in Asia, welcoming students from every corner of the world.

His lessons are built for two kinds of surfers: those catching their first wave, and those ready to push into faster sections and sharper positioning. Beyond the bay, Lahiru guides intermediate surfers along the reef and beach breaks stretching from Madiha and Mirissa through Weligama, Midigama, Ahangama and Kabalana, the same stretch of coast he grew up surfing.

Surfing in Weligama

Weligama means "sandy village," and its wide, curved bay is exactly that: a long stretch of sand wrapped by two headlands that soften almost every swell before it reaches shore. The waves peel slowly and predictably left and right off a sandbar, giving beginners time to find their feet and intermediate surfers a forgiving wall to practice turns on.

The main season runs roughly from November to April, when south-west monsoon swells wrap into the bay. The water sits warm year round, usually in the high twenties Celsius, so a rash guard is normally all you need. On smaller or off-season days, Weligama still holds a rideable wave more often than most of the south coast, which is why it has become Sri Lanka's most popular place to learn.

Questions about surfing here

Where is Lahiru Prasad based?

Lahiru teaches in Weligama, on Sri Lanka's south coast, and guides surf tours along the coastline from Madiha and Mirissa through Weligama, Midigama, Ahangama and Kabalana.

How much do surf lessons cost in Weligama?

A private beginner lesson is 25 USD for 75 minutes, and a private intermediate lesson is 35 USD for 90 minutes in the water. Group lessons for 2 to 4 people run 20 to 30 USD per person. Every lesson includes a rash guard, a surfboard and a coconut.

Is Weligama good for beginners learning to surf?

Yes. Weligama's bay is sheltered by two headlands, which softens the swell into a long, slow, predictable beach break, one of the most forgiving waves to learn on in Sri Lanka.

Do the guided surf tours suit beginners?

The guided tours are for intermediate surfers who can already catch and ride a green wave. Lahiru takes small groups or solo surfers by tuktuk to reef and beach breaks around Madiha, Mirissa, Weligama, Midigama, Ahangama and Kabalana.

What is the best time of year to surf in Weligama?

The main season runs from November to April, though Weligama's sheltered bay holds a rideable wave more consistently than most of the south coast even outside peak months.

What should I bring to a surf lesson?

Just yourself and swimwear. Lahiru provides the surfboard and rash guard for every lesson, and a fresh coconut afterward.
